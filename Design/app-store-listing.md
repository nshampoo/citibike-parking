## Description (≤ 4,000 characters)

Never ride up to a full station again. Park It shows how many docks are open at every station near you, so you know where to park before you get there.

LIVE MAP
Every station is a pin with its open-dock count, colored green, amber, or red. Tap one for bikes, e-bikes, and docks, plus cycling directions in Apple Maps.

LOCK SCREEN WIDGET
Check docks without unlocking your phone. Show the stations closest to you, your favorites, or the ones near a place you're heading, then tap ↻ to refresh.

COMMUTE MODE
Set Home and Work once. In the morning the widget shows docks near Work; after noon, near Home. No fiddling.

PLACES AND FAVORITES
Star the stations you use most and save places you ride to. Tap a place to see open docks around it before you arrive.

SIRI AND SHORTCUTS
"How many docks at 72nd in Park It?" or "Docks near Work in Park It."

OPEN DOCKS FILTER
Hide full stations with one tap.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location never leaves your phone.

Park It works with Citi Bike stations in New York City and Jersey City, using Citi Bike's public station data. Park It is an independent app and isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Citi Bike or Lyft.

## Review notes (for App Review, not shown publicly)

Park It shows live dock availability for Citi Bike, which operates only in New York City and Jersey City. If you're testing elsewhere, the app detects that you're outside the service area and shows Manhattan instead. To test as a local, set the simulator/device location to 40.7781, -73.9820.

No account or login is needed. Location permission is optional; without it the app shows a default area in Manhattan.

The Lock Screen widget: long-press the Lock Screen → Customize → add "Park It". Siri: "How many docks at W 72 St & Broadway in Park It".
