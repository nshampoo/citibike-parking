Common questions

The Lock Screen widget shows old numbers. iOS decides how often widgets refresh (roughly every 15 minutes at best). Tap the ↻ on the widget, or open the app, to refresh right away.

The widget says "Set Home & Work in the app". Commute mode needs both. Tap the Set Home and Set Work chips in the app.

Siri doesn't recognize my phrase. Say "How many docks at station in Park It" or "Docks near place in Park It". Siri can take a few minutes after installing to learn new phrases.

I'm not in New York. Park It covers Citi Bike's service area (New York City and Jersey City). Elsewhere, it shows New York.

Park It is an independent app and isn't affiliated with Citi Bike or Lyft. Station data comes from Citi Bike's public feed.
