Privacy Policy

Last updated September 24, 2026

Park It doesn't collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Location

With your permission, Park It uses your location to sort nearby Citi Bike stations and to show them in the Lock Screen widget. Your location is used only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Favorites and places

Stations you star and places you save (like Home and Work) are stored only on your device, so the app and its widget can read them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

To show live dock counts, the app downloads Citi Bike's public station feed. That request doesn't include your location or any information about you.

Searching for a place to save, opening directions, and displaying the map use Apple's MapKit and Apple Maps, which are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Park It with Siri or Shortcuts, requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Park It only answers them on your device.

Children

Park It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
